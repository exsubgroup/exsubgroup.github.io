📋 How Subscriber Tasks Work

3

4

📸 Required Screenshots (4 milestones)
1
Initial Screenshot (0 sec)
Pause video at start · Do NOT subscribe yet · Show video title + player at 0:00
2
Like Screenshot (anytime during watch)
Click 👍 Like · Take screenshot showing the Like button active (blue)
3
Subscribe + Bell Screenshot (after watching some)
Click Subscribe + press 🔔 Bell · Screenshot showing both icons
4
Final Screenshot (near end)
Post comment · Take screenshot at last 5–10s · Red playback line near end · Comment + Like + Subscribe + Bell all visible
⏱️ Watch at least 95% of the video · Take screenshots at each milestone · Upload all of them together when you submit proof
